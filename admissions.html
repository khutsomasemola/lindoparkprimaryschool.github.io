Before You Apply

A little preparation goes a long way.

Before contacting the school, take a moment to make sure you have the basic information needed to start the conversation.

Know the grade Be clear about the grade for which the learner is enquiring.
Have contact details ready Make sure your phone number and email address are current.
Prepare your questions Write down anything you would like the school to clarify.
Confirm official requirements Requirements and dates should be confirmed with the school before submitting documentation.
Keep copies of submissions Keep a record of information and documents you submit.
